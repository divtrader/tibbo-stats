Tibbo Stats: privacy

Tibbo Stats is a small private dashboard for the Tibbo YouTube channel (@tibboshow). It is used only by the channel's owner.

  • It reads the Tibbo channel's own YouTube statistics, read-only: views, watch time, and totals by country, traffic source and device.
  • It never reads, stores or shows anything about individual viewers. YouTube only gives channel totals.
  • It never changes anything on YouTube and never posts anything.
  • The channel totals are saved as a small file on this website so the dashboard can show them. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • The channel owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tibbo Stats' use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
